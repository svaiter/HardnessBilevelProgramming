- **E-1:** Proposition 2.5 fails for general semialgebraic functions in dimension at least two: reciprocal distance to the diagonal is unbounded even away from the origin. It holds in dimension one, or with boundedness on bounded sets. Use corrected A3, with bound shaped as \(B+(1+\sum_i x_i^2)^M\). The cited source was not checked; polynomials need no such general growth result.
- **E-7:** The compact-convex class requires nonempty \(Y\); otherwise its value can be identically \(+\infty\).
- **E-8:** A minimizer-based value graph requires attainment. Without it, characterize finite values by a universal lower-bound condition and approximation for every positive \(\varepsilon\).
- **E-11:** Theorem 2.12 does not ensure unique lower minimizers. “Any selection” applies exactly for real-valued \(h\); pessimistic results require negation. Its optimistic construction reverses infinite signs pessimistically.
- **E-28:** Exact degree five needs \(r\ge1\) for \(Q\), \(k\ge1\) for \(P\); degree at most five suffices for Theorem 3.2.
